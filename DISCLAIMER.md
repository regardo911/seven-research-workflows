## Synthetic data only

Every row of every fixture in this repository is fabricated. The sample IDs, the
patient IDs, the sites, the counts, the dates and the RIN scores were generated by a
script. No real applicant, patient, participant or specimen appears anywhere in this
repo, and none should be added to it.

## What is not tested here

`agentskills validate` checks the four Skills against a frontmatter contract. It does
not check their judgment, and neither does anything else in this repository. A Skill
that validates is a Skill whose `name` and `description` are well formed. Whether it
fires when it should, and whether the report it produces is right, is something you
find out by running it on your own work and reading the output.

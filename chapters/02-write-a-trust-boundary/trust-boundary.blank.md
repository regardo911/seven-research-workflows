# trust-boundary.md — <your subfield, your assay>

List the ten to fifteen computational tasks in your actual week. Not aspirational
tasks. The ones you did last week. Sort each into one of the two tables below.
Minimum five rows each.

The gate on the first table: **every unsupervised row must carry a detection method
that resolves outside the model.** A count, a checksum, a resolvable identifier, a
control's presence, a statistic you can recompute by hand on a subset. If the only
way you would catch the error is "I would probably notice", the row belongs in the
second table.

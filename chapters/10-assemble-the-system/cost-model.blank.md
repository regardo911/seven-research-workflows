# The cost model, blank

Put the date you last filled this against real logged time at the top, and refill it
when you write your next progress report rather than reusing it. A model that is
honestly two quarters old and says so is fine. Presenting a stale model as current is
the same category of error as a citation with the wrong year.

```
COST / TIME MODEL — one quarter (13 weeks)
Last filled against logged time: ____________
Loaded hourly rate used: $____/hr   [source: ________________________]

RECURRING TASK                    FREQ      HRS BEFORE  HRS AFTER  HRS SAVED/QTR
                                                                   ----------
                                              TOTAL HOURS SAVED / QUARTER
                                              VALUE AT $____/hr

TASKS ADDED BY THIS SYSTEM (be honest, these are real)
                                                                   ----------
                                              TOTAL HOURS ADDED / QUARTER
                                              COST AT $____/hr

TOOLS DISPLACED

SUBSCRIPTION

NET / QUARTER:
```

Rules for filling it:

- Every row cites something: an invoice, a published rate, or hours from your own
  calendar. Mark anything that is a guess as an estimate, in the model itself.
- **The negative section must not be empty.** Writing Skills is real work, running the
  rubric is real work, reading a reconciliation every week is real work. An empty
  negative section means you have not been honest, and a reader will find it first.
- Per quarter, never per year.
- Claim the glue, not the science. "This removed roughly six hours a week of format
  wrangling and environment repair" is credible, checkable, and boring in the way true
  things often are. "This accelerated our research" is not.
- Concede the ceiling out loud, before anyone else does. It is a layer on top of
  existing services. It needs domain knowledge to use properly. It will confidently
  produce something wrong if you let it. Then point at your rubric.

Show it to one person before you show it to the person who matters, and ask them which
line they do not believe. That line is the one that will be challenged, and you get to
fix it in private.
